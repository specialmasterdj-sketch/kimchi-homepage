Who We Are

Grocery shopping should feel warm and simple

At Kimchi Mart we believe grocery shopping should feel warm, simple and enjoyable. Our stores are clean, organized and filled with flavors from all across Asia — curated for families, food lovers, home cooks and everyone who enjoys good food.

From affordable everyday essentials to authentic Asian ingredients, we bring the comfort of home and the excitement of discovery together under one roof.

We opened our first store in 2019. Today we serve South Florida from Miami to Fort Lauderdale, with a sixth store opening in West Palm Beach this winter.
